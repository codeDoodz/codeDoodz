An interactive journey inside a transformer

The Attention Loom

Every chatbot you have talked to runs on the same machine. Scroll to watch a transformer take a sentence apart, weave its words together with attention, and guess what comes next. Nearly everything here moves, and much of it is computed live by a real, tiny transformer trained for this page.

The Attention Loom. Hand-built with vanilla JavaScript, Canvas and a lot of thread. No libraries, no tracking, no network calls.

The live model is a small decoder-only transformer trained from scratch in this repository on a toy world of sentences. Its weights ship with the page and every forward pass runs in your browser.

Further reading: Vaswani et al., Attention Is All You Need (2017) · Elhage et al., A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits (2021).
